## 5. hreflang (Sample: Shah Alam)

Every page emits 4 tags. Example for `/en/table-chair-rental/shah-alam`:

```html
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en" href="https://tablechair-rental-malaysia.vercel.app/en/table-chair-rental/shah-alam" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="ms" href="https://tablechair-rental-malaysia.vercel.app/ms/table-chair-rental/shah-alam" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="zh" href="https://tablechair-rental-malaysia.vercel.app/zh/table-chair-rental/shah-alam" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="x-default" href="https://tablechair-rental-malaysia.vercel.app/en/table-chair-rental/shah-alam" />
<link rel="canonical" href="https://tablechair-rental-malaysia.vercel.app/en/table-chair-rental/shah-alam" />
```

Rules for Kimmy:
1. Every one of the 117 pages emits its own 4-entry hreflang block (self-reference included).
2. `x-default` always points to the `en` variant of that specific slug.
3. `canonical` always points to the current URL (self-canonical).
4. Sitemap also includes `<xhtml:link>` alternates for every URL set.
5. hreflang values are `en`, `ms`, `zh` — **not** `en-MY`, `ms-MY`, `zh-MY`. Google accepts both; we keep it simple and avoid region-restricting the brand in Singapore/Indonesia searches where the MS/ZH audience overlaps.

## 6. Content Requirements for Nana

### 6.1 Homepage (per locale) — ~900–1100 words of body copy

Required elements:
1. **H1** (exact formula, Section 3.1) — includes primary keyword + price anchor.
2. **Hero sub-headline** — 1 sentence, mentions same-day delivery + nationwide + brand.
3. **Price teaser** — visible RM3.60 badge.
4. **ServiceArea intro paragraph** (60–90 words) naming all 13 states + phrase "kenduri, majlis perkahwinan, corporate event".
5. **ProductGrid**: for each of the 6 core products, a 40–60 word blurb using the product keyword at least once. Plain + with-cover pricing stated in text (not just in image alt).
6. **AdditionalRentals**: 7 short blurbs, 20–30 words each, each mentioning the add-on keyword once.
7. **Three-step process**: 3 steps × 25–35 words. Step 1 must contain "WhatsApp". Use imperative voice.
8. **Gallery intro** (40–60 words) + Google review branding label ("From real Google reviews" / "Dari ulasan Google sebenar" / "来自真实Google评价").
9. **FAQ block on homepage** (optional but recommended) — 4 brand-level questions (delivery area, deposit policy, minimum quantity, cancellation).
10. **Meta title + meta description** (Section 3.1).

**Why each matters:** H1 + product blurbs cover the primary + all 13 secondary keywords on a single page so Google understands the site's core entity. Price teaser improves CTR. The state-mention paragraph helps the homepage rank for long-tail national queries without cannibalising location pages.

### 6.2 Location page (per locale × 38 slugs) — ~550–750 words body copy each (UNIQUE)

Required elements:
1. **H1** — exact formula, Section 3.2.
2. **Unique intro paragraph** (120–180 words) — MUST mention:
   - Primary keyword (1–2 times, natural)
   - The specific city name (3–5 times)
   - One geographic hook unique to that city (neighbourhood, a famous venue, road landmark, or hosting tradition). Nana: no two cities get the same hook.
   - Same-day delivery phrase in the current locale.
   - WhatsApp CTA anchor.
3. **ProductGrid** — same 6 blurbs as homepage, but the intro line of each card is rewritten to mention the city once (e.g., "Banquet chairs delivered across {City}").
4. **AdditionalRentals** — reuse homepage copy (boilerplate is fine here; products are the same).
5. **Three-step process** — identical to homepage.
6. **Customer gallery** — identical to homepage.
7. **Location FAQ — 5 unique Q&As per page.** FAQ topics per location (pick 5 from this rotating pool, ensuring no two cities get identical sets):
   - Delivery coverage inside {City} (which suburbs / how far from the city core)
   - Same-day cutoff time for {City} orders
   - Minimum order quantity for {City}
   - Is the deposit refundable if the {City} kenduri is cancelled
   - Do you set up chairs and tables on-site in {City}
   - Are chiavari chairs available for {City} weddings
   - Can you combine canopy + chair + table packages for {City}
   - Do you deliver to apartment / condo halls in {City}
   - Is PA system delivery available in {City}
   - How far in advance should I book for weekend events in {City}
8. **Meta title + meta description** — Section 3.2.
9. **Image alt text** — every hero + gallery image mentions `{city}` + product type.

**Why each matters:** Google's duplicate detection penalises thin location pages. The 120–180 word unique intro plus 5 unique FAQs crosses the "materially different content" threshold. Neighbourhood + landmark references surface in voice-search and "near me" intents. 5 FAQ entries also feed the FAQPage schema for rich results.

### 6.3 Total copy volume for Nana
- 3 homepage copy sets (EN/MS/ZH) × ~1000 words = 3,000 words
- 114 location pages × ~650 words = ~74,000 words
- 114 × 5 FAQ pairs = 570 unique Q&A pairs
- 117 meta title + meta description pairs = 234 strings

## 7. Schema Markup Requirements (Kimmy)

All JSON-LD lives in `components/ui/SchemaMarkup.tsx` and is injected via helpers in `lib/schema.ts`. Emit one `<script type="application/ld+json">` per schema type per page (do not bundle into a `@graph` unless needed for size).

### 7.1 Per page type

**Homepage (all 3 locales):**
1. `Organization` — legal name, logo, sameAs (WhatsApp link), contactPoint.
2. `LocalBusiness` (subtype `PartyRental` via `additionalType: https://www.productontology.org/id/Party_equipment_rental_service`) — name, address (Shah Alam HQ), telephone, priceRange `RM3.60-RM86.40`, areaServed (13 states), geo, openingHours.
3. `WebSite` with `SearchAction` (even though no site search — allows future Sitelinks search box eligibility).
4. `BreadcrumbList` (Home only).
5. `ItemList` listing all 38 location pages as `ListItem` → enables sitelinks.
6. `FAQPage` (optional 4-question homepage FAQ from Section 6.1 #9).

**Location pages (all 114):**
1. `LocalBusiness` — SAME HQ address (Shah Alam), but with `areaServed` = the city, and `@id` unique per page (use the URL as `@id`).
2. `BreadcrumbList` (Home → Locations → City).
3. `FAQPage` — exactly 5 Q&A entries matching the visible FAQ block.
4. `Product` × 6 core products — `name`, `image`, `offers` with `priceCurrency: MYR`, low/high `price`, `priceValidUntil`, `availability: InStock`, `brand: Kak Kenduri`, `areaServed: {City}`. Critical: each `Product` must have `@id` scoped to the location page URL (`#product-banquet-chair`) so Google does not collapse the 228 product entries (6 × 38) into one.
5. `ItemList` for the 24 gallery images (`ImageObject` entries) — optional, boosts image-search traffic.
