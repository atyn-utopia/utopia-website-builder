**Section eyebrow:** `FAQ`
**Section H3:** `Wall Panel Questions, Honestly Answered`

1. **Eyebrow:** `PRICING`
   **H4 (Q):** `How much does wall panel installation cost per sqft in Malaysia?`
   **A:** Standard Wall Panel finishes (Wood, Fluted, PVC, Acoustic) start from **RM25/sqft**. Marble Wall Panel finishes (Gold, Silver, Black) start from **RM38/sqft**. Both prices include free installation, hidden-clip mounting and clean-up. Final cost depends on total square footage and any custom cuts around outlets or windows.

2. **Eyebrow:** `FREE INSTALL`
   **H4 (Q):** `Is free installation actually free, or are there hidden charges?`
   **A:** Free installation is fully included in the per-sqft price you see. No "labour from RM", no surprise transport fee, no after-hours surcharge. The only extras you might be quoted are major carpentry work or surface repair if your existing wall needs prep before panels can mount — and we always show that as a separate line so you can choose.

3. **Eyebrow:** `LEAD TIME`
   **H4 (Q):** `How long does wall panel installation take?`
   **A:** Most living rooms and bedrooms install in 1–2 days. Boardrooms, reception walls and larger spaces usually take 3–7 days end-to-end including measurement and finishing. We confirm an exact install window in your written quote so you can plan around it.

4. **Eyebrow:** `COVERAGE`
   **H4 (Q):** `Do you install wall panels outside the Klang Valley?`
   **A:** Yes — we install across Malaysia including KL, Selangor, Johor, Penang, Ipoh, Negeri Sembilan, Melaka, Pahang, Kedah, Kelantan, Terengganu, Perlis, Sabah and Sarawak. Outstation jobs are quoted at the same per-sqft promo price; transport is included for orders above 200 sqft.

5. **Eyebrow:** `STYLES`
   **H4 (Q):** `Which wall panel style works best for my space?`
   **A:** As a rough guide: Wood and Fluted suit living rooms and bedrooms; PVC suits kitchens, bathrooms and humid yards; Acoustic suits home offices, studios and meeting rooms; Marble suits luxury feature walls, reception areas and boardrooms. We bring real samples on the free measurement visit so you can see the texture under your actual lighting before committing.

6. **Eyebrow:** `DURABILITY`
   **H4 (Q):** `How durable are your wall panels?`
   **A:** All our panels are 12mm pre-finished and mounted on hidden clips — stronger and more service-friendly than glued installs. With normal care, expect 8–10 years of life on Wood and Marble panels, and longer on PVC and Acoustic. Workmanship warranty is included in writing.

7. **Eyebrow:** `MAINTENANCE`
   **H4 (Q):** `How do I clean and maintain a wall panel?`
   **A:** Wipe with a dry microfibre cloth weekly. For PVC and Marble panels you can use a damp cloth with mild soap. Avoid harsh thinners or wire brushes on Wood and Acoustic finishes. We send a one-page care sheet with every install.

8. **Eyebrow:** `BOOKING`
   **H4 (Q):** `How do I book a free measurement?`
   **A:** Just WhatsApp us a photo of your wall, the rough dimensions and the style you're considering. We confirm a free measurement slot within the same day, send the installer over at the time you choose, and follow up with a written quote with the promo price locked in.
